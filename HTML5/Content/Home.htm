Explore Accessibility

This is Baby Corn! He is an internet cat celebrity (you can find him on Instagram on the account @whiskstowhiskers). He's a lot smaller than all the other cats his age, and he has a neurological condition that causes him to struggle with balance. But he is still a happy and healthy cat who loves helping others! He and his humans foster kittens and senior cats who need a place to go before finding forever homes, or who need a retirement home in their golden years. Even though he has a hard time playing rough with larger cats and needs a bit of extra support sometimes, he still loves helping others (currently he's working on his personal finance journey and expanding his budget). His person was kind enough to let me use his likeness for this project, and I hope he can help spread the good word about online accessibility!

This site was created as a final project for English381 in the Professional Writing and Technical Communications program.

Accessibility is an effort by every team member. It takes each person caring about users of all abilities to make sure that the final products meets the standards of usability. There are a lot of excuses about accessibility that come up when a product doesn't meet those standards and doesn't function for all users. But as creators and designers of things that people rely on as they go through the world, we have an obligation to make sure our products aren't exclusive to just the "ideal" users. Ensuring this level of quality starts with the first draft and doesn't end until we've made a product that does its job for everyone.

Visit the About This Project page for more information about this project.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sources did you use for this project?

You can visit the Works Cited page for each content section under the section header tab.

How important is accessibility online?

Very! In an online world, it's just as important as physical accessibility infrastructure in real life. Visit our page on Why does Accessibility Matter? to learn more about why it is important to include accessibility in online design.

Who is this website for?

The primary audience for this project is designers and developers (you can visit pages in each section on including accessibility features using the software Clipchamp), but anyone who wants to learn more about accessibility and relevant features is welcome to browse around!
